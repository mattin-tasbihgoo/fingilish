Fingilish Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 13, 2026

Fingilish is a phonetic Persian keyboard for iOS. This policy covers the

Fingilish app and its keyboard extension.

The short version

Nothing you type ever leaves your phone. Fingilish collects no data,

sends no data, and has no servers.

What the keyboard does

When you type a word in Latin letters and press space, Fingilish converts

it to Persian script using a 28,000-word dictionary that is embedded inside

the app on your device. All conversion happens locally. The keyboard:

  • does not request "Full Access" (iOS's switch for keyboards that want

network or shared-data capabilities) — it is designed to run entirely

without it, and iOS itself blocks network use for keyboards without it;

  • does not collect, store, or transmit anything you type;
  • does not use analytics, advertising identifiers, or tracking of any

kind;

  • contains no third-party data collection. (The open-source keyboard

engine we build on links a crash-reporting library, but it is never

activated in Fingilish — no crash reports are generated or sent.)

What the app stores on your device

The app keeps a small amount of local data so the keyboard works:

keyboard resource files, your فا/EN mode preference, and an internal flag

recording which resource version is installed. This data stays in the

app's private container on your device and is deleted when you delete the

app.

Data collected: none

Under Apple's privacy questionnaire, Fingilish's answer is **"Data Not

Collected."** There is nothing to sell, share, or breach — we never have

your data in the first place.

Children

Fingilish does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this

URL with a new effective date. Any change will never reduce the core

guarantee above: what you type stays on your phone.
