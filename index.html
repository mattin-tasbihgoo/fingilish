Fingilish

Type Persian the way it sounds. salam + space → سلام

Fingilish is an iOS keyboard for everyone who speaks Persian but types in English letters. Type each word phonetically and it converts to Persian script as you go — powered by a 28,000-word dictionary that lives entirely on your phone.

No Full Access. No accounts. Nothing you type ever leaves your device.
